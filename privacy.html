Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

When you place an order or contact us, we collect necessary personal details including your name, delivery address, phone number, and email address to process and fulfill your purchase efficiently.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your details are used exclusively to process transactions, dispatch packages through verified courier partners, provide tracking notifications, and offer customer support assistance.

3. Local Storage & Cookies

WATCHOX uses client-side LocalStorage technology to remember your shopping cart items, wishlist preferences, applied coupons, and recently viewed products. We do not sell or trade your browsing history to third-party ad networks.

4. Data Sharing & Courier Partners

We share relevant shipping details (such as recipient name, contact phone number, and address) with logistics partners solely for fulfilling package delivery and cash collection.

5. Security Assurance

We employ modern browser storage standards and standard encryption protocols to safeguard your order information. Your payment confidence and data privacy remain top priorities for WATCHOX.

6. Updates to Policy

We may periodically update our privacy policies to reflect operational or legal adjustments. Revisions become effective immediately upon being published on this page.
