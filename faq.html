Orders & Delivery

How does Cash on Delivery (COD) work? +

With Cash on Delivery, you pay in cash directly to the courier representative upon receiving your package. You are allowed to inspect the outer packaging and parcel prior to completing payment.

What are the delivery charges and timelines? +

Delivery inside Dhaka takes 24–48 hours, while delivery outside Dhaka takes 2–4 business days. Standard delivery is ৳80, but orders above ৳7,000 qualify for FREE shipping automatically.

How can I track my shipment? +

Once your order is dispatched, you will receive an Order ID. You can enter your Order ID and Phone Number on our Track Order page to see real-time status updates.

Authenticity & Warranty

Are all WATCHOX timepieces 100% authentic? +

Yes. Every watch sold on WATCHOX is guaranteed 100% authentic and genuine. We source directly from authorized manufacturers and verified global distributors.

What does the official warranty cover? +

Our official warranty covers manufacturing defects in the movement, hands, and dial mechanism for 1 to 2 years (depending on the brand model). Physical damage, water damage beyond limits, or normal strap wear are excluded.

Returns & Exchanges

What is your return policy? +

We offer a 7-day hassle-free replacement warranty if you receive a damaged, defective, or incorrect product. The item must be unused with its original box, tags, and warranty card intact.
